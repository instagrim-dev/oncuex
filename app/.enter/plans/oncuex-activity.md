# Resolve issues from Overview

Keep producers on Overview while they investigate and act on the specific issue they selected. Reuse the existing project record, review rules and sample workflow; do not rebuild the binder or imply that a fictional source is a real confirmation.

- [ ] Replace each “Fix this” navigation link with an in-place, issue-specific resolution interface. Show the current values, relevant findings and supporting sources, then offer the appropriate action for access, capacity, schedule, budget, attestation or supporting-document issues.
- [ ] Make each actionable Next Up item open the same kind of focused interface for its own check, including its owner, dependencies and all remaining findings. Open questions should offer an appropriate unsent clarification or review action, not be silently marked answered.
- [ ] Where a modeled plan change can address an issue, let the producer review and explicitly apply validated values from within Overview. Distinguish a proposed or manually entered value from an approved or externally confirmed fact, retain available source references, and show the potential effect on related work and sign-off before applying it.
- [ ] For missing, expired, outdated or inconsistent evidence, present the relevant source and available review or replacement actions without rewriting historical records. Do not treat an unsupported excerpt, an expired document or a missing fire attestation as verified merely because someone clicks a button. If necessary information or a supported action is unavailable, explain what is still needed rather than claiming a resolution.
- [ ] Keep prerequisite problems, secondary findings and unresolved issues visible. After an action, recompute the existing checks and show what changed and what still needs attention; an accepted concern is not a plan change or sign-off.
- [ ] Preserve explicit role checks, revision conflicts, historical sign-offs, local persistence and existing error handling. Failed or invalid actions must not be presented as successful, and no messages or external actions are sent.
- [ ] Verify the affected in-place flows and existing change/recovery behavior, including invalid inputs, evidence limitations, remaining blockers and sign-off currentness. Report any interaction or persistence checks that cannot be exercised.
